# POS Upgrade Roadmap

Current system mein products, categories, sales, purchases, inventory, customers, suppliers, users/roles aur settings already mojood hain. Neeche practical upgrades priority ke saath diye gaye hain.

## 1. Dashboard Reports

- Aaj ki sales, profit, expenses aur low-stock products
- Daily, weekly aur monthly charts
- Top-selling products aur cashier-wise sales

## 2. Better POS Experience

- Barcode scanner support
- Product search aur keyboard shortcuts
- Held ya suspended bills
- Partial payments aur credit sales
- Receipt print/download
- Sale cancel, refund aur exchange workflow

## 3. Inventory Improvements

- Low-stock alerts
- Stock transfer between branches
- Physical stock count aur adjustment
- Product variants: size, color aur brand
- Batch number, expiry date aur serial number tracking
- Purchase cost ke basis par real profit calculation

## 4. Customer aur Supplier Accounts

- Customer udhaar aur ledger
- Supplier payable ledger
- Payment history aur due-date reminders
- Customer purchase history

## 5. Security aur Audit

- Detailed role-based permissions
- Sale edits, refunds aur stock adjustments ka audit log
- Cashier shift opening/closing
- Daily cash reconciliation

## 6. Hardware Store Features

- Units: piece, meter, kg, liter aur box
- Product aliases aur barcode labels
- Quotation ya estimate banana
- Quotation ko sale mein convert karna
- Wholesale customers ke liye custom pricing

## 7. Technical Quality

- Forms mein complete Zod validation
- Automated tests
- Better error pages aur loading states
- Database backup aur restore
- Production deployment configuration
- Unreliable internet ke liye PWA ya offline support

## Recommended First Phase

Sab se pehle dashboard reports, barcode POS, customer/supplier ledger aur refunds/returns implement karne chahiye. Ye features daily business operations ko sab se zyada improve karenge.
